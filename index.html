WOC Customer Support Project

Using Data, CRM, and AI to Improve Customer Service

Problem Solution Project Labs Team

The Business Problem

WOC currently receives customer questions through multiple channels, including phone calls, emails, Instagram DMs, and in-store conversations. This can make it difficult for employees to respond quickly and consistently, especially during busy periods.

Our Proposed Solution

Our team recommends implementing a CRM and AI-assisted customer support system. The system would organize customer interactions and data from different communication channels, helping employees provide faster and more consistent support.

CRM

Organize customer interactions and information in one central system.

Data Analytics

Identify customer trends, common questions, and service bottlenecks.

AI Support

Assist employees with customer questions while keeping humans involved.
